BRAND AND COMPLIANCE STANDARDS
Revision 1

This file is injected verbatim into every reviewer's context. It is the shared
standard the council measures a document against. Edit it to match your own
organization; the council reads it at runtime and does not cache it.

1. SUBSTANTIATION
   Every claim about capability, performance, or outcome must be traceable to
   a stated source. A claim with no source is a finding, regardless of whether
   it happens to be true.

2. ABSOLUTES
   Do not state that anything is guaranteed, permanent, universally secure, or
   incapable of failure. Bound the claim: what conditions, over what period,
   measured how.

3. SUPERLATIVES
   Comparative claims ("leading", "best in class") require a named comparison
   set and a date. Without both, remove the comparison rather than soften it.

4. NAMING
   Use the approved terms: agent, council, reviewer, finding, verdict, ledger.
   Do not introduce new product nouns in a document that has not been through
   naming review.

5. CONTROL LANGUAGE
   Describe what a control does, not what it prevents. "Requires an approver
   before merge" is verifiable; "prevents unauthorized changes" is a claim
   about outcomes that the control alone cannot support.

6. PERSONAL DATA
   If a document places personal data in scope, it must state the lawful
   basis, the retention period, and the named owner. All three, or none of the
   document ships.

7. IRREVERSIBILITY
   Any action that cannot be undone must say so plainly and name who authorizes
   it. Do not describe an irreversible action in the same register as a
   reversible one.
